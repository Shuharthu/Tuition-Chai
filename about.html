Our Mission in Chattogram

"টিউশন ও টিউটর এর জন্য নতুন সম্ভাবনা"

Tuition Chai was born out of a simple conviction: finding a dedicated, qualified tutor in Chattogram shouldn't be stressful, insecure, or clouded by predatory agency commissions.

Transforming EdTech in Bangladesh

In traditional tuition markets across Chattogram—from Panchlaish and Nasirabad to Halishahar and GEC—both guardians and university students face friction. Parents worry about student safety and unverified qualifications, while talented university students from CUET, CU, and CMC lose up to 60% of their first month's earnings to middlemen agencies.

Tuition Chai replaces opaque middlemen with a modern, technology-first marketplace. We enforce stringent academic verification (Student IDs, board examination results, institutional enrollment) while giving guardians a direct, safe platform to review mentors and track ongoing classes.
